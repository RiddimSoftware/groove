# Customer Experience (CX) Library

Distilled canon for the **Customer Team** (the `customer-team` skill; Linear team `Customer` / `CUS`). Pull from this on demand when a CX cycle needs a supporting principle or the scoring rubric — do not bulk-load it.

This is the CX analogue of [`../architecture/`](../architecture/) (Clean Architecture) and [`../aso/`](../aso/) (the ASO playbook): the seminal literature distilled into agent-legible chapters, plus the derived scorecard the skill scores each product against.

## What's here

- `cx-scorecard.md` — the **0–10 CX rubric**. Start at 10.0, deduct for service-quality smells. Grounded in the four books below. This is what a CX Pulse cycle scores each product against — the CUS analogue of `../architecture/architecture-scorecard.md`.
- `chapters/` — chapter-by-chapter distillations. Each chapter follows the org template: **Core Principles · Enforceable Rules · Review Questions · Examples · Implications**.

### The canon (`chapters/`)

| Book | Pull it when you need… |
|---|---|
| `winning-on-purpose/` — Reichheld, *Winning on Purpose* (2021) | The **north star**: loyalty as the purpose of the business ("enrich the lives of customers"), Net Promoter 3.0 / Earned Growth, the closed-loop system. Loyalty framing and which signal a recommendation should move. |
| `the-effortless-experience/` — Dixon, Toman & DeLisi (2013) | The **operating doctrine**: the Customer Effort Score, reduce-effort-beats-delight, repeat-contact and channel analysis. Triaging friction and prioritizing complaint signal. |
| `SERVQUAL/` — Parasuraman, Zeithaml & Berry | The **bedrock**: the Gaps Model and the **RATER** dimensions (Reliability, Assurance, Tangibles, Empathy, Responsiveness). The basis of the scorecard rubric. |
| `hbr-shostackpdf/` — Shostack, service design & blueprinting | Journey and touchpoint mapping — locating *where* in the experience a complaint originates. |

## Known gaps (intentionally deferred)

Identified as part of the CX canon but **not yet distilled** — by decision, not oversight. Add them when the team needs them:

- **Jobs-to-be-Done** (Christensen, *Competing Against Luck*) — would sharpen how a feature request becomes a Linear Customer Need.
- **Jay Baer, *Hug Your Haters*** — the public-complaint / review-response discipline.

> A fifth folder, Bryan Magee's *Ultimate Questions* (a philosophy book), was removed: it was distilled by mistake through a title collision with Reichheld's *The Ultimate Question 2.0*. NPS / loyalty is already covered by `winning-on-purpose/` — do not re-add it.
