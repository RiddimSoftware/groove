# Chapter 5: To Get Control, You Have to Give Control

## Core Principles
- The most important factor in a frontline rep's performance is their Control Quotient (CQ)—the ability to handle high-pressure situations, exercise judgment, and maintain resilience.
- CQ cannot be trained via scripts; it must be enabled by a work environment built on trust, alignment, and peer support.
- Managing for strict Average Handle Time (AHT) and using exhaustive QA checklists severely limits a rep's ability to reduce customer effort.

## Enforceable Rules
- Eliminate the checklist mentality in Quality Assurance. Evaluate reps on broad competencies and customer outcomes (like making it easy) rather than scripted behaviors.
- Remove the pressure of time. Suppress AHT at the rep level and focus instead on Available Talk Percentage (ATP) to encourage reps to spend the time necessary to resolve issues.
- Build a strong peer support network. Enable reps to share best practices and learn from each other in unstructured or peer-moderated environments.

## Review Questions
- Does our QA process encourage reps to act like robots, or does it evaluate their ability to ease the customer's effort?
- Do reps feel trusted to use their judgment to resolve complex, unique customer issues?
- Are reps aligned with the company's service mission, understanding how their daily actions contribute to broader goals?

## Examples
### Violation
- A rep interrupts a frantic customer to strictly follow a QA checklist requirement of verifying a zip code, increasing customer frustration.
### Good Implementation
- A company abandons its 26-point QA checklist in favor of 5 core loyalty behaviors, allowing reps the freedom to handle the call organically as long as they reduce the customer's effort.

## Implications
### For Agents
- Systems should provide contextual information to reps but step out of the way of dictating the exact flow of the conversation.
### For Tickets/PRs/CI
- Reporting tools should de-emphasize AHT in favor of metrics that track issue resolution and peer-to-peer knowledge sharing.
