# Chapter 7: Making Low Effort Stick

## Core Principles
- Transitioning to a low-effort organization requires a cultural shift, not just a short-term campaign or flavor-of-the-month initiative.
- Effort reduction is an experientially learned skill (experience engineering, judgment) and must be coached, not just trained in a classroom.
- Over-reliance on formal classroom training hurts performance compared to integrated, on-the-job coaching.

## Enforceable Rules
- Develop a compelling "change story" that contrasts the old, checklist-driven way of doing things with the new, effort-reduction philosophy.
- Replace punitive "scheduled coaching" (reviewing bad calls from days ago) with "integrated coaching" (real-time, on-the-floor coaching).
- Do not make effort reduction just "another ask." Remove conflicting expectations, like strict handle times, to give reps the space to focus on effort reduction.
- Start small. Focus initial pilots on specific tactics, like reframing "no" (positive language) or forward-resolving one specific issue type.

## Review Questions
- Are supervisors spending the majority of their coaching time on the floor, side-by-side with reps, rather than in scheduled, punitive reviews?
- Have we clearly communicated what reps are *no longer* expected to do in order to free them up to reduce effort?
- Is effort reduction treated as a core service expectation or just a checklist item?

## Examples
### Violation
- Management adds a new field to the QA scorecard for "Customer Effort" without removing any existing requirements or AHT targets, causing reps to view it as just another box to check.
### Good Implementation
- A company uses a "Customer Effort Flight Simulator" where reps role-play difficult scenarios with peers, learning network judgment and positive language in a safe, unscripted environment.

## Implications
### For Agents
- Onboarding systems and training modules should emphasize interactive, scenario-based learning rather than rote memorization of procedures.
### For Tickets/PRs/CI
- Implement features that facilitate peer-to-peer feedback and real-time coaching support on the floor.
