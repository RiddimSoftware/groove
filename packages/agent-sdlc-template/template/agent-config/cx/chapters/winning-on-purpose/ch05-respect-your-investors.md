# Chapter 5: Respect Your Investors

## Core Principles
- There is no tension between loving customers and delighting long-term investors. In fact, investors win *only* when customers are loyal.
- Financial accounting is outdated and often fails to capture the true value of a company's customer base. "Customer-based accounting" is necessary to measure the real health of the business.
- A new metric, the Earned Growth Rate (EGR), is essential for gauging progress. It measures revenue growth generated by existing customers returning and bringing their friends.
- EGR consists of two components: Net Revenue Retention (NRR) and Earned New Customer Revenue (ENCR).

## Enforceable Rules
- Never rely solely on self-reported, unaudited NPS numbers for benchmarking against competitors, as they are prone to bias. Use double-blind relationship scores.
- Transition accounting systems to track customer-based metrics: onboarding date, reason for joining (referral vs. marketing), purchase frequency, and defection date.
- Evaluate company health by whether it beats a diversified market index (like Vanguard's VTI). If a company isn't beating the index, it isn't delivering true value to its investors.

## Review Questions
- What percentage of our growth is "earned" (from loyal customers and referrals) versus "bought" (through marketing and promotions)?
- Does our accounting system track the lifetime value and retention of our customers, or does it only track aggregate revenue?
- Are we reporting NPS accurately, or are we using biased, transaction-based surveys to inflate our numbers for investors?

## Examples
### Violation
- Companies that report inflated, self-administered NPS scores in their IPO filings or annual reports without explaining the methodology, misleading investors about the true state of customer loyalty.
### Good Implementation
- First Republic Bank presenting an investor slide showing exactly what percentage of their deposit and loan growth came from existing customers and their referrals, inspiring the concept of Earned Growth Rate.

## Implications
### For Agents
- When building reporting or analytics dashboards, ensure that the Agent includes capabilities to track customer cohorts, referral sources, and retention rates, rather than just raw traffic or aggregate sales.
### For Tickets/PRs/CI
- Analytics implementations in PRs must capture attribution data accurately (e.g., distinguishing between a user who signed up via a referral link vs. a paid ad) to support Earned Growth Rate calculations.
