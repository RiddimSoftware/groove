# Chapter 1: Lead with Love

## Core Principles
- The primary and unbeatable purpose of a great organization is to enrich the lives of its customers.
- There is a distinction between "good profits" and "bad profits". Good profits are earned by creating Promoters and enriching lives, whereas bad profits result from exploiting customers.
- Embracing this purpose is not only a moral choice but also a highly effective business strategy that builds resilience and sustainable growth.
- Customer capitalism prioritizes the customer's interests above those of all other stakeholders, which ultimately benefits everyone, including investors.

## Enforceable Rules
- Never use the Net Promoter Score (NPS) simply as a target or metric to punish employees. It must be used as a measure to inspire learning and growth.
- Do not engage in "bad profit" practices such as tricking customers, nuisance fees, or predatory pricing.
- Recognize and reward team members who successfully enrich customers' lives and "get caught doing the right thing."

## Review Questions
- Does this decision or feature genuinely enrich the life of the customer?
- Are we extracting value from our customers, or are we creating value for them?
- How does our team feel on the inside? (Because how they feel is how they will treat customers on the outside).

## Examples
### Violation
- A car dealership uses manipulative negotiation tactics and then begs/threatens the customer to give them a perfect 10 on the NPS survey to avoid corporate punishment. This makes the survey a tool of fear rather than a tool for improvement.
### Good Implementation
- Apple Retail stores start their day with a "daily download" huddle focused on customer feedback. They celebrate employees who receive Promoter comments, using NPS to inspire the team and improve the customer experience daily.

## Implications
### For Agents
- AI Agents must focus on creating solutions that genuinely solve user problems and "turn frowns into smiles." The goal is not just functional completion but enriching the user's experience. Avoid dark patterns or value-extracting behaviors in code.
### For Tickets/PRs/CI
- PRs should be evaluated on whether they make the product more delightful and easier to use for the customer.
- Metrics and CI checks should not become blind targets (like the car dealership's NPS) but should serve to provide honest feedback to the developers to improve the product.
