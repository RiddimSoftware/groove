# Chapter 6: Store Promotional Tools & Customer Information

Recent changes by Apple and Google have introduced new marketing features that allow developers to promote their apps dynamically in and around the stores. 

## Store Promotional Tools

### Pre-Registration Pages
*   **App Store Pre-Order:** Can be set 2 to 180 days before launch. Users are notified and the app auto-downloads on release.
*   **Google Play Pre-Registration:** Can last up to 90 days. Developers can offer a free in-app gift as a reward for pre-registering.

### Giving Customers a Foretaste
*   **Google Play Instant:** Allows users to try a partial version of an app from the store listing without fully downloading it (max 15MB total).
*   **Apple App Clips:** A small app sample (max 10MB per clip). Unlike Google, App Clips are discovered *outside* the App Store via Safari banners, iMessage, NFC tags, QR codes, and Apple Maps. 

### Promoting Specific App Content
*   **Google Play Custom Store Listings:** Target users based on their specific country or install state (e.g., pre-registration users). Max 5 custom listings. Great for country-specific offers within a shared language locale.
*   **App Store Custom Product Pages (iOS 15):** Allows up to 35 custom pages per app with different screenshots, videos, and promotional text. Accessible via dedicated URLs. Ideal for targeting specific user intents (e.g., male vs. female audiences, different sports in a sports app, or offline QR code campaigns).
*   **Google Play LiveOps Cards:** Promote limited-time events, major updates, and offers on the store listing. *Note: Not indexed for search.*
*   **App Store In-App Events:** Event cards are heavily featured and **are indexed** in App Store search results. Adding valuable keywords to the Event Name and Short Description can temporarily boost rankings.
*   **Promoting In-App Purchases (App Store):** IAPs can appear directly in search results. Their 30-character display names are indexed, providing extra keyword targeting space.
*   **App Store App Bundles:** Bundle up to 10 apps. The bundle name (up to 85 characters) is indexed.

### Store-Run Marketing Programs
*   **Google Play Points:** A loyalty program where users earn points for store purchases, which can be reinvested in participating apps.
*   **Google Play Pass & Apple Arcade:** Subscription services offering premium access. Play Pass covers both apps and games, whereas Apple Arcade is exclusively for games. Arcade games feature a unique product page layout (auto-playing video banner) and have no ratings displayed.

## Customer Information Tools

### Data Privacy Sections
*   **Apple's iOS Nutrition Labels:** Developers must disclose data collected and linked to the user vs. data not linked. Appears near the bottom of the product page, thus having a minimal direct impact on conversion rates.
*   **Google Play Privacy Information Cards:** Similar "Data Safety" section requiring developers to declare data collection, usage, and sharing practices.

### Search Adjustment Tools
*   **App Store Search Autocorrect & Suggestions:** Apple autocorrects misspelled queries and suggests related topics at the top of the search results to refine user intent.
*   **Google Play Search Filters:** Users can filter by tags, "Premium", "Editor's Choice", or "Star Rating". *Warning:* Google Play's star rating filter relies on the **daily average** of new ratings, not the historical average. A 4.6-star app could be filtered out if its daily average drops below 4.5 on a given day.
