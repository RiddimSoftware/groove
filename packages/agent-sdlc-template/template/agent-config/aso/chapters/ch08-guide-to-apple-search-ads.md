# Chapter 8: Guide to Apple Search Ads (ASA)

Apple Search Ads (ASA) has become essential for scaling visibility and generating high-intent traffic. Due to the interconnected nature of the App Store, running ASA correctly leads to organic uplift (higher keyword rankings and conversion rates).

ASA launched in 2016 and, as of February 2022, is available in **61 storefronts**. Since May 2021, ASA also runs in the **Search tab** (pre-query placement above suggested apps) in addition to traditional search results. The ASA Stack framework was developed by Andrea Raggi and the Phiture team.

## The ASA Stack
A strategic framework consisting of 5 layers to master ASA:

### 1. Campaigns
*   **Structure:** Organize by semantics per market (e.g., Brand, Generic, Competitor, Discovery, Probing). 
*   **Budget & Daily Caps:** Use high lifetime budgets to avoid unwanted pauses. Use Daily Caps to throttle spend on probing campaigns and increase scale on high-ROAS campaigns.
*   **Storefronts:** Target specific high-converting countries to optimize performance, or add multiple storefronts to achieve scale.

### 2. Ad Groups
*   **Targeting:** Refine by Device (iPhone vs. iPad), Customer Type (New vs. Returning), Demographics (Age/Gender), Location, and Ad Scheduling. Keep settings broad to scale; narrow them to optimize ROAS.
*   **Intent Segmentation:** Separate ad groups based on intent grades. Use Single Keyword Ad Groups (SKAGs) for top-performing keywords to gain maximum bidding control.
*   **Custom Product Pages (CPPs):** Link ad groups to specific CPPs (e.g., matching a generic fitness keyword to a yoga-specific landing page) to maximize conversion from ad tap to download.

### 3. Keywords
*   **Match Types:** 
    *   *Exact Match:* Precise targeting for performance.
    *   *Broad Match:* Targets related terms and misspellings.
    *   *Search Match:* Matches ad groups to search terms algorithmically without explicit keywords. Excellent for discovery.
*   **Bids:** Set maximum bids on top keywords for scale. Lower bids on poor performers to optimize CPA.

### 4. Tools
*   **Automation:** Scripts to automate bidding and keyword redistribution.
*   **MMPs & ASA API:** Mobile Measurement Partners (like Adjust/AppsFlyer) track post-install events (purchases/trials) to optimize for ROI rather than just CPI.
*   **App Store Connect:** Essential for monitoring organic cannibalization.
*   **Third-Party Tools:** E.g., SearchAdsHQ for full media buying funnel analysis.

### 5. Supporting Insights
*   **Organic Uplift:** Successful ASA campaigns increase total download velocity, often boosting organic rankings for those same keywords.
*   **Micro & Macro Influences:** Endogenous factors (e.g., Share of Voice) and Exogenous factors (e.g., seasonality, market shifts).
*   **Cannibalization:** When an ad buys a download that would have happened organically anyway (frequent with Brand keywords). 
    *   *Analysis:* Monitor if organic Search App Units drop while ASA New Downloads spike. 
    *   *Cost Modeling:* Calculate the "Cost Per Protected Install" by comparing your ad spend against the estimated conversion rate of the competitor trying to steal your brand term. This helps determine if defending the keyword is financially viable.
