# Chapter 3: Increasing Conversion

While KWO drives impressions, Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) turns those impressions into actual downloads. Without a stable or improving conversion rate, top-line impression gains will not yield long-term success.

## Main Considerations for CRO
Conversion rates are measured differently on the two stores:
*   **App Store Metrics:** 
    *   *Click-Through Rate:* Page Views (unique) / Impressions (unique)
    *   *App Page Conversion Rate:* First-Time Downloads / Page Views (unique)
    *   *Search Conversion Rate:* First-Time Downloads / Impressions (filtered by Search). This is the purest metric for ASO.
*   **Google Play Metric:** 
    *   *Store Listing Conversion Rate:* Store Listing Acquisitions / Store Listing Visitors.

### Benchmarks & Element Prioritization
A "good" conversion rate varies widely by category. Selected App Store benchmarks (AppTweak, US, 2021):

| Category | App Page CR | Install Rate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Shopping | 47.6% | 12.4% |
| Utilities | 60.5% | 10.5% |
| Finance | 42.4% | 10.7% |
| Photo & Video | 78.6% | 6.5% |
| Health & Fitness | 53.9% | 4.0% |
| Travel | 11.6% | 5.3% |
| Magazines & News | 35.5% | 0.3% |

A high CTR with low page CR (e.g., Travel) means users browse a lot but rarely commit. A high page CR with low CTR (e.g., Photo & Video) means the product page is strong but the search-result preview is weak. Always compare against your *category benchmark*, not raw numbers.

When prioritizing which elements to optimize first, industry consensus points to the "big three" visual elements: **App Icon**, **Screenshots**, and **App Video**. Text metadata is generally less impactful for conversion (but crucial for keyword indexation — only ~2% of App Store users and ~5% of Google Play users read the full description).

Reported average CVR uplifts from A/B testing platforms (methodology varies):
*   **SplitMetrics (App Store):** Icon ~22.8%, Screenshots ~21.7%, Video ~16.6%.
*   **SplitMetrics (Google Play):** Screenshots ~24.3%, Icon ~20.2%, Video ~17.4%.
*   **Storemaven:** Video drives the biggest impact (App Store ~40%, Google Play ~24%), screenshots ~28%/13%, icons ~18%/11%.
*   **Title/text:** ~8% (Storemaven) to ~14.4% (App Store, SplitMetrics) — meaningful but smaller than visuals.

## Phiture's CRO Loop Framework
A systematic approach to CRO involves six steps:

### Step 1: Conducting Research
*   **Looking Inward:** Scrutinize your app's true Value Proposition (benefits vs. mere features), analyze feature usage data, and mine user reviews for pain points and desired outcomes. Ensure brand alignment.
*   **Looking Outward:** Monitor competitors, best-in-class apps, and broader ASO best practices. Consider the **PET-ASO Model** (Persuasion, Emotion, Trust) to design creatives that trigger motivation and mitigate user doubts.
*   **Looking Skyward:** Account for seasonality, market intelligence, and global affairs.

### Step 2: Hypothesizing
Don't just test randomly. A good hypothesis specifies:
1.  **Action:** What you are changing.
2.  **Result:** What you expect it to do.
3.  **Reason:** Why, based on your research.

### Step 3: Prioritizing
Use the **RICE** scoring model to prioritize your hypotheses: Reach, Impact, Confidence, and Effort.

### Step 4: Creating Store Assets
*   **App Icon:** Critical for Google Play search. Incorporate branding, utilize clear identifiers, and stand out. Follow strict new Google Play policies (no emojis or "best" claims).
*   **Title & Subtitle/Short Description:** Front-load important keywords to avoid truncation. Craft compelling copy that explains the value prop immediately.
*   **Screenshots:** Display the most important features first. Experiment with styles like connected screenshots, visual pop-offs, or custom backgrounds.
*   **Video (App Preview / Promo Video):** Show the main value immediately. Use captions for sound-off viewing. Ensure the pace keeps attention.
*   **Other Elements:** Optimize the Full Description (using HTML formatting on Google Play), leverage the "What's New" section to show active development, utilize Developer Pages, and promote In-App Purchases (App Store) with descriptive names. Utilize **Visual Word Recognition**—mirroring top searched keywords visually in your creatives.

### Step 5: Testing: Running A/B Tests & Experiments
Ensure a homogeneous cohort and account for seasonality. Avoid testing too many variables at once.
*   **Bold vs. Incremental:** Bold tests find big gains faster but carry more risk. Incremental tests are safer but require more time to show significant uplift.
*   **Testing Tools:** Use Google Play Store Listing Experiments (native A/B testing), Apple's Product Page Optimization (native iOS 15+), third-party A/B testing tools, or sequential (before-and-after) analyses.

### Step 6: Reporting & Analyzing
Rely on data rather than hunches. Monitor the correct KPIs (Install rate, retention). Be wary of external noise (like paid campaigns) skewing organic conversion data. Use statistical methods like Causal Impact Analysis (paired t-tests) to confirm that the changes genuinely moved the needle.
