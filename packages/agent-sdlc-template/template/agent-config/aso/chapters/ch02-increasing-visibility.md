# Chapter 2: Increasing Visibility

Improving visibility is foundational to ASO. Visibility comes from four main sources: **Search**, **Feature placements**, **Browse & Explore**, and **Store Ads**.

## 1. Increasing Search Visibility
Keyword Optimization (KWO) is most effective for apps addressing a specific, well-defined market need (e.g., "period tracker" or "puzzle game"). 

### Understanding the Search Algorithm
The algorithms evaluate three core factors:
1.  **Indexation (Eligibility):** Is the keyword in your metadata? Title (highest weight on both stores), Subtitle (App Store), Short Description (Google Play), Keyword Field (App Store, hidden), Long Description (Google Play, indexed; Apple does not rank from long description), Developer Name, Package Name (Google), IAP names (App Store), In-App Event names (App Store), Bundle names (App Store).
2.  **Relevance:** How heavily is the placement weighted, and how prominently does the keyword appear (front-loaded vs. buried)?
3.  **Ranking Strength:** Based on historical data — primarily downloads & download velocity, conversion rate for the keyword, user retention for the keyword (Google Play especially), and ratings/reviews.

*Notes:*
*   **Apple's plural/compound matching is imperfect**, especially in non-English locales. When in doubt, include both forms (`podcast` and `podcasts`). For `mice` vs. `mouse`, matching fails entirely — you must include both.
*   **Apple gives new apps a "Week-Long Keyword Boost"** in their first 7 days post-launch. Launch with your strongest metadata in place to capitalize.
*   **"Free Ranking Keywords" (App Store):** Apple indexes some terms without them appearing in metadata — `iPad`, `iPhone`, `app`, `apps`, `free` (if free), primary/secondary category names, most misspellings, most singular/plural variants, sometimes root↔variant pairs. Don't waste character budget on these.
*   **Google uses neural-network skip-gram models** to associate related terms beyond literal text. Apple's algorithm is simpler.

### The Keyword Optimization Cycle
KWO is a continuous, iterative 4-step process:

#### Step 1: Keyword Research
Create a backlog of potential keywords using:
*   Brainstorming
*   Competitor keyword analysis
*   Store Autofill suggestions
*   Apple Search Ads terms
*   Google Keyword Planner
*   Long-tail keyword shuffling
*   User reviews

#### Step 2: Keyword Prioritization
Filter your backlog by analyzing:
*   **Volume:** E.g., Apple's Search Popularity score (exponential, 5-100 scale).
*   **Relevance:** Does the search intent align with your app?
*   **Competition (Difficulty):** How many apps target the term, and how strong are the top 10 apps? Calculate difficulty manually by checking competitor ranks and volatility, or use ASO tool difficulty scores.

#### Step 3: Targeting Keywords in Metadata
Implement top priority keywords strategically:
*   **Title:** Carries the most weight (30 characters max). Use exact matches. Do not keyword stuff to the detriment of branding.
*   **Subtitle (Apple) / Short Description (Google):** Avoid repeating title keywords. Use high-relevance terms.
*   **Keyword Field (Apple):** 100 characters. Separate with commas, no spaces. Use singulars. Don't repeat words.
*   **Long Description (Google):** 4,000 characters. Density matters. Place crucial keywords in the first few lines.
*   **Other Metadata:** Developer name, In-App Purchase names, Event names, and Package name (Google Play) are also indexed. 
*   **Visual Word Recognition:** Reflect your targeted keywords in your visual assets to boost conversion rates.

#### Step 4: Measuring Keyword Performance
Track rankings using ASO tools. Key metrics include:
*   Keyword Ranks (aim for Top 10)
*   Impressions (Apple) / Store Listing Visitors (Google)
*   Installs / First-Time Downloads
*   Keyword-level User Retention
*   Organic Uplift (installs gained beyond a baseline or paid efforts)

## 2. Increasing Visibility Through Getting Featured
Getting featured by Apple or Google editors can skyrocket visibility. 

*   **Google Play Types:** Editors' Choice, Hero Banner, New/Updated Apps, Collections.
*   **App Store Types:** Editorial Stories, App/Game of the Day, Collections.

### How to Get Featured
*   **Build an Outstanding App:** Focus on high UI/UX quality, stability, strong ratings, and rapid adoption of new OS features (e.g., Dark Mode, iOS Widgets).
*   **Strong Positioning & Storytelling:** Stand out with a unique value proposition. Provide compelling behind-the-scenes stories to editorial teams.
*   **Nurturing Relationships:** Build a respectful, helpful rapport with store representatives. 
*   **Pitching:** Use a short, visually appealing pitch deck (3-4 slides). Highlight unique features, KPIs, engagement metrics, and major upcoming marketing plans.

### Impact of Being Featured
Featurings directly increase Browse/Explore impressions. "App of the Day" yields significantly higher results than being placed low in a list-style collection. Relevant featurings drive better conversion rates. Be prepared for a post-featuring drop-off, relying on KWO for long-term growth.

## 3. Increasing Browse Visibility Outside of Featurings
*   **Top Chart Rankings:** Calculated largely by download velocity (and revenue for Top Grossing). Pick your app's primary category strategically based on relevance, user intent, and competitiveness.
*   **Google Tags:** Select up to 5 highly relevant tags to help Google classify your app for "Similar Apps" and recommendations.
*   **Burst Campaigns:** Generating massive volumes of (often incentivized) downloads in a short burst to climb Top Charts quickly. *Warning:* Highly discouraged by platform guidelines.
*   **Category Switching:** Moving to a less competitive category if the app fits, to achieve higher ranks.
*   **Similar Apps:** Being suggested at the bottom of a competitor's app page. Highly driven by algorithms utilizing tags and keywords.
