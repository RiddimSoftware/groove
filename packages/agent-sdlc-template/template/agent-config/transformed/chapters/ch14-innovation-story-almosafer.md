# Chapter 14 — Innovation Story: Almosafer

The first of several **innovation stories** — case studies of what becomes possible *after* a company has successfully moved to the product model.

## Company Background

Almosafer is a regional travel company in Saudi Arabia. It transformed to the product model with the help of one of the book's authors (Christian Idiodi) and built a strong empowered product team capability.

## The Problem to Solve

In the Middle East, particularly Saudi Arabia, families have a tradition of renting *istirahas* — private retreats, typically for the afternoon to evening of the same day. There was no established online marketplace for finding and booking them.

**Key risk**: this was a new solution to a new problem (most products are new solutions to existing problems). Both the solution had to be discovered, *and* the market had to be developed — the region had to be educated about this new type of offering.

## Discovering the Solution

- The team partnered closely with **business development and marketing** to identify istirahas willing to list in the marketplace, ensuring supply.
- In parallel, they discovered the necessary customer experience for those looking to book.
- The team worked with product coach **Hope Gurion** on the effort.
- COVID forced them remote; the team's existing discovery skills and trust let them transition quickly to remote collaboration and remote customer research — covering both problem and solution discovery.

## The Results

- Substantial unknowns at the outset: nobody knew how many istirahas existed, let alone how many owners would list, let alone how many bookings would occur.
- **Initial business goal**: demonstrate product-market fit for the marketplace offering, which the team quickly established.
- Focus shifted to **growth**. Istirahas became an integral and rapidly growing category.
- Beyond financial success, this capability served the country and region in a time of need — *"few in the region have the illusion that any of the global travel companies would have cared enough to have invested anything."*
- Today Almosafer has **70+ percent market share** in the region.

The extraordinary result is the product of a sustained effort of talent and technology to truly care for customers.

## Lessons

- The combination of strong product, design, and engineering competencies enables tackling problems global incumbents won't touch.
- An empowered product team can navigate a pandemic-driven shift to remote without losing momentum — because trust and skills travel.
- Innovation isn't only about new technology; it's about **new applications** of existing technology to underserved markets.
