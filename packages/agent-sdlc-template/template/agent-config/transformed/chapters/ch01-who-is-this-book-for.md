# Chapter 1 — Who Is This Book For?

## Audience

*TRANSFORMED* is for anyone helping a company move to the product operating model:

- Members of product teams trying to move to the product model
- Product leaders guiding the company through these changes
- Senior executives, especially the CEO and CFO, who want to understand what's involved and decide whether to pursue it
- Other company executives, stakeholders, and employees impacted by the model
- Product coaches helping companies transform

## The "Tech Company" Confusion

The most common gating question: *"We're not a tech company, so is the product model even relevant for us?"*

The book's answer reframes "tech company": **it refers not to what a company sells but to how the company powers its business with technology.**

- Tesla sells cars
- Netflix sells entertainment
- Google sells advertising
- Airbnb sells lodging
- Amazon sells everything

What unites them is not their industry; it is how they design, build, and run their business. The product operating model is for *any* company that believes it should be powering its business with technology — today, that's nearly every industry.

## Relationship to INSPIRED and EMPOWERED

- **INSPIRED** captured the best practices of strong product teams.
- **EMPOWERED** captured the best practices of strong product leaders.
- **TRANSFORMED** answers the question the first two left open: *how do you get there from where we are?*

You do not need to have read the earlier books. *TRANSFORMED* introduces the model from first principles.

## The Central Challenge

> "Our company operates so differently from what you describe. Is it even possible for a company like ours to change to the product model, and how would we change?"

This is the book's central question. One CEO described it as *"changing from driving on the right side of the road to the left — but gradually."* The shift is substantial and anything but easy, but it is possible.
