# Chapter 35 — Innovation Story: Datasite

The innovation companion to the longer Datasite transformation story in Part VII. Where Part VII tells the story of how Datasite transformed, Chapter 35 illustrates what became possible afterward.

## Company Background

Datasite (formerly Merrill Corporation) provides secure data rooms used by investment banks, lawyers, and other deal advisors during M&A transactions, IPOs, and similar deals.

It's a category that requires:
- Extreme security (deal-sensitive documents)
- Workflow capabilities for many parties to collaborate without inappropriate access
- Reliability under intense deal pressure
- Compliance with legal and regulatory regimes globally

For decades, the category was dominated by document-management thinking. Datasite transformed to the product model and reset what its product could do.

## The Problem to Solve

The status quo: dealmakers spent enormous time on the mechanics of running deals — uploading documents, managing access, answering routine questions, tracking what counterparties had seen.

With AI and modern product capabilities, an enormous part of this work could be automated, freeing humans to focus on the deal itself.

## Discovering the Solution

The empowered product teams at Datasite, equipped with the discovery skills from transformation, identified several leverage points:
- **AI-powered document analysis** — flagging key terms, identifying gaps
- **Workflow automation** — repetitive steps handled by the platform
- **Predictive insights** — what counterparties are likely focusing on
- **Better collaboration** — across the many parties to a deal

Each direction was tested with real users (dealmakers and their counterparties) before significant build.

## The Results

Datasite emerged with capabilities that significantly extended the category — and that competitors couldn't quickly match because they hadn't built the empowered product capability.

The story illustrates several themes:
- A B2B enterprise SaaS company can be a vivid example of product-model innovation
- Industries with deep domain complexity (M&A) are not exceptions to the product model — they benefit from it disproportionately
- AI capability becomes a force multiplier when applied through empowered product teams that understand the workflow and customer

## Lessons

- **Domain complexity** doesn't exempt a company from the product model — it amplifies the value of empowered teams who can navigate that complexity in partnership with users.
- **AI is not a strategy** by itself. It's a capability that empowered teams apply to specific customer problems. The discovery / delivery muscles are what convert AI capability into customer value.
- **Transformation pays for itself** when external technology shifts (AI, in this case) make new products possible.
